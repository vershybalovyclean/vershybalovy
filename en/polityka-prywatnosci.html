2. Scope and purposes of data processing

We process Clients' personal data for the following purposes:

  • fulfilling orders and providing cleaning services (basis: necessity for the performance of a contract – Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR),
  • contact in matters related to the service (basis: legitimate interest – replying to enquiries),
  • fulfilling tax and accounting obligations (basis: legal requirement – Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR),
  • possible establishment of claims or defence against claims (basis: legitimate interest).

Scope of data: first name, surname, address, telephone number, e-mail address, address where the service is performed, description of the scope of work, and other data provided by the Client when placing the order.

In connection with running the Partner Programme we also process data of persons applying as Partners (first name and surname, telephone number, e-mail address, type of cooperation) in order to handle the application, assign a partner code and settle the commission due (basis: necessity for the performance of a contract – Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR and tax and accounting obligations – Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).

3. Recipients of data

Data may be disclosed to:

  • entities providing accounting, legal and IT services (e.g. website hosting),
  • public authorities entitled to request it under the law.

4. Transfers of data outside the EEA

We do not plan to transfer personal data to countries outside the European Economic Area unless this is necessary to perform the service (e.g. when using cloud tools based outside the EEA – in that case we apply appropriate safeguards, e.g. EU standard contractual clauses). Data is stored on servers in the European Union.

5. Data retention period

Data is stored for the period necessary to perform the service and then for the period required by law (e.g. tax law – 5 years from the end of the tax year), unless the Client consents to longer storage.

6. Clients' rights

Every Client has the right to:

  • access their data,
  • rectify data,
  • erase data (the "right to be forgotten") – unless it is necessary to perform the contract or to comply with a legal obligation,
  • restrict processing,
  • data portability,
  • object to processing,
  • withdraw consent (if processing is based on consent) at any time.

To exercise these rights, please contact us at kontakt@vershclean.pl.

7. Complaints

The Client has the right to lodge a complaint with the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (PUODO) in the event of a breach of the GDPR.
