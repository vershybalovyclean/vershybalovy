Scope of services

Version of 31.01.2026

← Back to the home page

1. Scope of the maintenance service (basic)

  • Tidying and putting things in order,
  • Vacuuming floors and carpets,
  • Mopping floors,
  • Cleaning and disinfecting the bathroom / WC,
  • Cleaning the sink, taps and kitchen worktop,
  • Cleaning the hob (surface),
  • Dusting furniture,
  • Cleaning mirrors and glass surfaces,
  • Taking out the rubbish.

2. Scope of the general service

Includes all activities from item 1 and additionally:

  • Removing cobwebs from walls and ceilings,
  • Cleaning sockets and switches,
  • Cleaning wall decorations and pictures,
  • Cleaning tiles by the sink and hob,
  • Cleaning kitchen cabinet fronts,
  • External cleaning of appliances,
  • Vacuuming upholstered furniture,
  • Cleaning skirting boards,
  • Cleaning radiators (externally),
  • Cleaning the front door on the inside,
  • Cleaning interior windowsills.

3. Scope of the detailed service (detailed / deep)

Includes all activities from items 1 and 2 and additionally:

  • Cleaning radiators inside (steam cleaner),
  • Removing limescale from shower cabin glass,
  • Cleaning ventilation grilles,
  • Cleaning lamps and light shades,
  • Degreasing kitchen cabinet handles,
  • Cleaning the space under the hob,
  • Moving the washing machine and cleaning underneath it,
  • Removing rust and limescale from the bottom of the toilet,
  • Cleaning and polishing interior doors,
  • Cleaning curtain rails and air conditioners,
  • Polishing wooden furniture,
  • Changing bed linen.

4. Scope of the move-out cleaning service

Includes the General scope (item 2) and additionally:

Kitchen

  • Fridge – cleaning inside and outside,
  • Dishwasher – cleaning inside and outside (accessible places),
  • Oven – cleaning inside,
  • Extractor hood – cleaning inside and outside,
  • Kitchen cabinets – cleaning inside,
  • Removing grease stains.

Bathroom

  • Full disinfection of sanitary fittings,
  • Cleaning the toilet, bath / shower cabin,
  • Cleaning tiles and grout (surface),
  • Bathroom cabinets – cleaning inside.

Wardrobes (depending on floor area)

  • up to 60 m² – 1 wardrobe (cleaning inside and outside),
  • 61–90 m² – 2 wardrobes,
  • 90+ m² – 3 or more wardrobes.

Other

  • Cleaning doors and handles.

5. Scope of the post-renovation cleaning service

  • Dusting walls,
  • Dry cleaning of the floor from construction dust,
  • Preliminary floor washing,
  • Removing local construction stains from the floor,
  • Mechanical cleaning and washing of ceramic tiles to remove grout residue, cleaning grout lines,
  • Cleaning and washing skirting boards,
  • Cleaning and washing sockets and switches,
  • Cleaning and polishing light fittings and lamps,
  • Cleaning and washing sensors and other hanging elements,
  • Cleaning the boiler, meters and pipes,
  • Cleaning and washing electrical and water boxes,
  • Cleaning and polishing worktops, sinks and kitchen taps,
  • Unpacking and external dusting of appliances,
  • Cleaning and washing furniture fittings,
  • External and internal washing and polishing of kitchen cabinets,
  • External and internal washing and polishing of room furniture,
  • Cleaning and polishing furniture fronts,
  • Washing bed frames and slats, dry cleaning of soft parts,
  • Cleaning and polishing mirrors and glass surfaces,
  • Cleaning and polishing doors, trims and fittings,
  • Cleaning and washing bathroom walls of dust, grout and construction residue (full height),
  • Cleaning and disinfecting toilets and sinks and other sanitary fittings,
  • Final washing and polishing of floors.

Notes on post-renovation cleaning

  • New furniture and appliances (never used) – cleaned inside and outside.
  • Furniture and appliances already in use – cleaned externally and on open surfaces; internal cleaning is quoted individually.
  • Furniture soiled through everyday use – vacuumed / dusted externally.

6. Additional services (paid separately, to choose with each package)

a) Rooms

  • Cleaning light fittings / chandeliers,
  • Cleaning radiators,
  • Cleaning inside wardrobes,
  • Changing bed linen,
  • Arranging / sorting items,
  • Ironing,
  • Taking down / hanging curtains,
  • Cleaning the air conditioner (filters),
  • Cleaning the pet litter tray,
  • Cleaning surfaces at height (above 2.5 m),
  • Cleaning the balcony / terrace,
  • Moving furniture.

b) Bathroom

  • Cleaning grout between tiles,
  • Washing the floor (intensive),
  • Cleaning the washing machine (e.g. cleaning the filter, removing limescale),
  • Cleaning the ventilation grille,
  • Washing walls (full height),
  • Unblocking the trap / drain,
  • Cleaning the toilet (stubborn limescale / deposits),
  • Removing mould,
  • Removing limescale / rust,
  • Cleaning the shower drain / strainer,
  • Removing limescale from glass shower screens,
  • Full cleaning of the shower cabin.

c) Kitchen

  • Cleaning the kettle (inside and outside),
  • Cleaning the microwave (inside and outside),
  • Cleaning the multicooker / other small appliances,
  • Cleaning the electric grill,
  • Cleaning the fridge (inside and outside),
  • Cleaning the freezer (defrosting, washing),
  • Cleaning the oven (inside),
  • Cleaning the extractor hood (inside and outside),
  • Cleaning the dishwasher (inside, filters),
  • Degreasing kitchen cabinet fronts,
  • Cleaning kitchen cabinets (inside and outside),
  • Sanitary disinfection of the waste bin.

d) Other

  • Window cleaning (inside / outside, frames, sills, screens) – from 25 zł/m² (season 01.04–15.10) / from 40 zł/m² (winter 16.10–31.03); minimum order 199 zł.

7. Scope of the cleaning service after hoarding

The service covers the full process of restoring premises after hoarding or long-term neglect, carried out in stages:

  • Technical audit – on-site inspection, assessment of the scope of work, photo documentation, cost estimate (300 zł, deducted from the final price after the agreement is signed),
  • Sorting and removal – sorting items, packing valuables, removal of waste (from 800 zł),
  • Deep cleaning – steam cleaning, mechanical mould removal, disinfection of all surfaces (min. 1500 zł),
  • Odour neutralisation – ozonation, cold fog, odour neutralisers (from 300 zł),
  • Final cleaning – window cleaning, polishing surfaces, preparing the premises for habitation or sale (from 400 zł).

Due to the individual nature of each case, the final quote is set only after the technical audit has been carried out.

8. Additional information

  • Photo documentation – before and after the service (at the Client's request).
  • Invoice / receipt – for every order.
  • Service for rental management agencies – a fixed price list and a collective invoice under separate arrangements.
  • Surcharge for heavily soiled premises – from 10% to 15% (in accordance with item III of the Terms).
