Scope of work

What's included?

Move-out cleaning (after a tenant change or relocation) includes a full deep clean of the apartment or house, plus additionally:

🍽️ Kitchen

  • Fridge — cleaned inside and out
  • Dishwasher — cleaned inside and out (accessible areas)
  • Oven — cleaned inside
  • Extractor hood — cleaned inside and out
  • Kitchen cabinets — cleaned inside
  • Grease removal

🛁 Bathroom

  • Full disinfection of sanitary fittings
  • Toilet, bath / shower cabin cleaning
  • Tile and grout cleaning (surface)
  • Cabinets — cleaned inside

🗄️ Wardrobes

Additionally included — wardrobe cleaning inside and out:

  • up to 60 m² — 1 wardrobe
  • 61–90 m² — 2 wardrobes
  • 90+ m² — 3 or more wardrobes

FAQ

Frequently asked

How quickly can you come after tenants move out? ▼
We accept orders with completion within 24h of move-out. In summer season it's worth booking ahead — contact a manager and we'll check availability.
Do you regularly serve Airbnb apartments? ▼
Yes, we serve Airbnb and Booking hosts on an ongoing basis. We offer fixed pricing, priority scheduling and consolidated invoices for rental management agencies.
What does "without furniture — zone-based pricing" mean? ▼
For an empty apartment we can calculate the price based on the area of individual zones (kitchen, bathroom, bedroom, living room). This way you only pay for zones that actually need cleaning — no overpaying for empty rooms.
Do you take documentation photos? ▼
Yes, before/after photo documentation is part of the standard scope. We send photos to the owner or agency after work is completed.
Can I add ozone treatment to move-out cleaning? ▼
Yes, ozone treatment is an optional add-on — especially recommended when an unpleasant smell lingers from previous tenants or occupants (smoke, pets, damp). This applies to both apartments and houses. You'll find the price and how to add it in the additional services section. During ozone treatment, no people or pets may be present in the room. After the treatment, the room must be properly ventilated before anyone re-enters.
